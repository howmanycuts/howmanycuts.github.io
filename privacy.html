Privacy

How Many Cuts? is a free game that runs in your browser. There is no account and nothing to install.

What the game saves

Your best times, the fruits you unlocked, your language and the sound setting are saved in your browser, on your device only. They are not sent to us. You can erase them here.

Ads

The game may show banner ads from Adsterra to pay for itself. When ads are shown, Adsterra and its partners can use cookies and similar data (such as your IP address and device type) to choose and measure ads. In Europe, the game asks for your answer first and shows no ads if you say no.
